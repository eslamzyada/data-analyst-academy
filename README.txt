DATA ANALYST ACADEMY
====================

To open it: double-click "Start Data Analyst Academy" (in this folder), or the
"Data Analyst Academy" shortcut on your desktop. Your browser opens the app.

A small minimised window keeps the app running. Close that window when you're
done studying. Everything works offline.

Your progress is saved on this computer only, in:  app\data\academy.db
To keep a copy: open the app → Settings → Download backup.

Your work saves itself as you type ("Saved ✓" appears at the top right).
You can refresh the page, close the browser or restart the computer: half-typed
answers, quizzes, exams and projects are put back where you left them. The Home
page lists anything you have not finished.

The "old-version" folder is the earlier Markdown-based system, kept only as
an archive. The app doesn't use it, and you can ignore it.
